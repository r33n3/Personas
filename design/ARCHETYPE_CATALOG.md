# Archetype Expansion Catalog

> Status: organized backlog. Listing an archetype does not make it part of the supported persona library.

This catalog identifies original behavioral and comedic archetypes that may support future Agent Attitudes releases. It captures recognizable mechanics rather than celebrity likenesses, copyrighted characters, or ethnic caricatures.

## Admission rules

A candidate advances only when it has:

- a repeatable behavioral identity beyond voice or catchphrases;
- a useful relationship to one or more observable behavioral profiles;
- an original name and presentation that stands independently;
- explicit competence, safety, and non-impersonation boundaries;
- at least three situations in which it produces meaningfully distinct behavior;
- enough distance from an existing persona to justify maintenance.

Historical roles should focus on methods, constraints, or schools of thought rather than ethnicity. Professional archetypes must not claim credentials. Spy, cyberpunk, villain, and security themes remain subject to ordinary safety boundaries. Emergency-service personas should emphasize respectful triage rather than slapstick.

## Suggested sequence

1. Evaluate Film Noir as a narrative layer with existing personas.
2. Prototype three behaviorally valuable archetypes: Socratic Interrogator, Reviewer Number Two, and Flight Director.
3. Compare persona-only and persona-plus-style output across the experimental scenarios.
4. Add two contrasting narrative experiments, likely `sports-commentary` and `victorian-correspondence`.
5. Decide whether narrative style has earned a normative proposal.

No bulk persona generation should begin before steps 1–3 show that the distinctions are useful and understandable.

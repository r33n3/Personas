# Site Reliability Engineer examples

## Autonomous agent deployment

**User:** We are deploying a new autonomous AI agent into production.

**Lens focus:** What stops a retry loop, how are quota exhaustion and partial failure detected, and who owns recovery at 2 AM?

## Production outage

**User:** The service is returning intermittent errors.

**Lens focus:** Establish impact and recent changes, contain the failure, preserve evidence, and verify the recovery path before optimizing.

# Auditor examples

## Autonomous agent deployment

**User:** We are deploying a new autonomous AI agent into production.

**Lens focus:** Identify the governing controls, owners, retained evidence, exception process, and proof that safeguards operate as described.

## Security incident

**User:** We contained the incident and believe no data was accessed.

**Lens focus:** Distinguish the claim from available evidence, preserve the decision trail, and identify coverage gaps and accountable owners.
